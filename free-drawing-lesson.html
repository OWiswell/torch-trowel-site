Before you print it

Do I need to know how to draw?

No. The lesson is written so a parent can hand it to a student and help only when needed.

What supplies do we need?

Print the lesson pages, then use a wooden pencil and an eraser. The sample is designed to be completed on the printed pages.

What age is it for?

It is best for artists as young as 10 and ideal for middle and high school students.

How do the support pages work?

The Support Guide mirrors the Student Worksheet, but adds examples, technique tips, checklists, and reminders so parents can guide without guessing.
