Our Philosophy

How we Build our Curriculum

At Torch & Trowel, every lesson is built on a simple rhythm: learn something, then make something.

First Principles

We teach the why behind the what in drawing. Students don't just copy, they actually understand.

Screen-Free

No videos, no apps, no subscriptions. We prioritize tactile learning.

Easy to Follow

Step-by-step lessons make it simple for parents to guide and even simpler for kids to learn independently.

Low Prep, Low Materials

Grab and go simplicity. No expensive or complex supplies, no hours of preparation.

Printer-Friendly

Clean, black-and-white worksheets that can be reprinted again and again for ease of use.

Our field kits are designed for families—screen-free, low-prep, and easy to use—so you don't have to be an art expert to guide them. Kids can work independently, build real drawing skills, and grow in confidence along the way.
