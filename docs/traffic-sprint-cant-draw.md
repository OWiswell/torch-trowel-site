## Email copy

Subject: When your student says "I can't draw"

Preview text: The first mark is easier when the page gives them somewhere to begin.

Hi,

When a student says "I can't draw," I try not to treat it as the final truth.

Sometimes it means, "I don't know where to start."

That is a different problem.

A blank page asks a beginner to make too many decisions at once: What do I draw first? How big should it be? What if the first line is wrong?

Torch & Trowel lessons are built to lower that first step. Instead of asking students to invent the whole drawing from nothing, the page gives them a focused skill and a clear beginning.

The goal is not to make drawing mechanical. The goal is to help students begin, then build confidence through real practice.

If you want to try it with your student, start here:

`https://www.torchandtrowel.com/free-drawing-lesson?utm_source=email&utm_medium=nurture&utm_campaign=cant_draw`

Matt
