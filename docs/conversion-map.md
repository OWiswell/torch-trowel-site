# Torch & Trowel Conversion Map

Primary site path:

1. Visitor understands the curriculum promise.
2. Visitor downloads the free drawing lesson.
3. Visitor tries the lesson at the table.
4. Visitor understands how student pages and support guides work together.
5. Visitor buys Drawing Field Kit Vol. 1.

Canonical public pages:

- `/` - home and orientation.
- `/free-drawing-lesson` - lead capture.
- `/free-sample-v1` - thank-you and download.
- `/drawing-field-kit` - product decision and purchase.
- `/homeschool-art-curriculum-guide` - comparison and fit.
- `/start-here` - how to use the sample or kit.
- `/about` - credibility and trust.
- `/policies` - privacy, terms, and refunds.

Hidden or deferred pages:

- `/field-notes` - noindex until the article system is useful enough to publish.
- `/curriculum-sample` - noindex legacy/sample route.
- `/product-drfk01` - noindex delivery route for buyers.

Core events:

- `click_nav_free_lesson`
- `click_nav_buy_kit`
- `click_free_lesson_cta`
- `click_sticky_free_lesson`
- `click_buy_now`
- `click_sticky_buy_now`
- `click_view_product`
- `click_start_here`
- `click_curriculum_guide`
- `start_lead_form`
- `submit_lead_form`
- `open_pdf_preview`
- `download_free_sample`
- `download_product_files`

Weekly optimization ritual:

1. Review visits by page.
2. Review free lesson CTA clicks.
3. Review form starts versus form submissions.
4. Review thank-you page downloads.
5. Review buy button clicks.
6. Change only one meaningful thing per week.
7. Keep changes trust-safe: no fake urgency, no shame, no inflated claims.
